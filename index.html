How do I place an order?+

Choose a model, add it to your cart, and continue to checkout. Enter your name, phone number, city, and delivery location. Add any engraving request in the Order Note.

Delivery and payment+

Your delivery fee appears at checkout. ABA / KHQR is available for all locations. Cash on Delivery is available for Phnom Penh orders.

What happens after I pay?+

Upload your payment receipt and tap “I Have Paid.” If you pay in parts, upload every receipt. CHAKO LAB checks the received payment before approving your order. Once approved, tap “I Have Paid” again to complete your order.

Can I personalize my tumbler?+

Yes. Add the name, message, or logo request in the Order Note at checkout so our team can review your engraving details.
